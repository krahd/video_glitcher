# Native Linux recovery QA checkpoint

On 7 October 2026, a bounded normal-GUI pass verified the targeted playback/export recovery flows at source commit [`841c74d04009bb0ac34591ea4127722179495a0f`](https://github.com/krahd/video_glitcher/commit/841c74d04009bb0ac34591ea4127722179495a0f), tree `b5b630d67af2b4bb9914aac52cf159387c747e61`. Only newly generated, owned synthetic media was used. No application source, dependencies, system libraries, user media or public publishers were changed during the run.

This records observed behaviour on one isolated Linux desktop. It does not establish a supported shipping runtime, physical GPU use, leak freedom, all-codec coverage, cross-platform acceptance or redistribution clearance.

## Observed flows

- Load and moving preview, coloured pause, cancellation of a paused load picker, and accepted replacement after pause passed.
- A complete export saved and fully decoded. Re-selecting that output was refused; its SHA256 remained unchanged.
- Accepted replacement after completed full export and filename refusal resumed playback without an extra Play click.
- X cancelled an active full export while the guide was open. Closing the guide showed moving preview and cancellation feedback. The cancelled final file and private staging were absent after cleanup.
- One live recording in ONCE mode reached the first end at 196 frames. Play resumed the same output to 391 frames. Rewind then Play resumed it again to 586 frames. The saved recording fully decoded, with changing frames in all three segments.
- Controls could be hidden/restored. A subsequent live recording was cancelled without changing the prior saved output or input.
- After normal application closure, the app window was gone, the launcher had exited and no ffmpeg process was listed. The detached source worktree remained clean.

## Warnings and open observations

1. Paused Rewind changed status to “rewound” but retained the last displayed source frame/timestamp (7.958 seconds) until Play. Rewind+Play resumed from the start and captured correctly. This remains a visual-feedback follow-up, not a claim that paused seeking now shows a fresh first frame.
2. Optional GStreamer plugin warnings remain: missing `libcrypto.so.1.1`, a libsoup3 `g_once_init_leave_pointer` symbol mismatch, and a libnice resource-path warning. The exercised local H.264 path worked despite these warnings. Other codecs/plugins and clean-machine delivery are unaccepted.
3. JOGL's shutdown diagnostic listed three open X11 display connections. No native-memory or resource-leak profiling was performed. Zero log lines matching “disposed” in this run does not establish leak freedom or universal warning elimination.
4. The short finishing-export cancellation race and deliberately injected native preview-restoration exceptions were not separately fault-injected through the GUI. Their automated coverage remains distinct from this native run.
5. No macOS/Windows/Alpine acceptance, physical-GPU claim, audio-export capability, packaging/signing/store result or licence/redistribution clearance follows from this checkpoint. Existing runtime/provenance and paid-redistribution holds remain.

The inherited queued-next-load presentation issues tracked in [PR #6](https://github.com/krahd/video_glitcher/pull/6) also remain open: next-load status can replace cancellation feedback, and an immediate failed next load can have its error overwritten by “Saved export; loading ...”. They were not repaired by the native QA pass.
